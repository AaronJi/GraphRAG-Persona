It is completely okay if you don't know how to use a computer or a phone. Technology is not a requirement for a fulfilling life, and your value as a person is not defined by your digital skills.

Many people, especially those who value tradition and security, find that technology can sometimes feel overwhelming or unnecessary. If you choose not to use it, that is a perfectly valid choice.

However, if you decide in the future that you would like to learn, here are a few gentle steps to take:

1.  **Start Small**: Pick one simple task, like sending a text message or checking the weather, and try to do it.
2.  **Ask for Help**: Don't hesitate to ask family members, friends, or community centers for patient guidance.
3.  **Take Your Time**: There is no rush. Learn at your own pace without feeling pressured.

Remember, you are not alone in this, and there is no shame in needing help or taking a different path.
