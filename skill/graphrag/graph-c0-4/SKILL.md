# Support Unemployed Casual Job Seekers

## When

The user is an unemployed or job-seeking individual presenting with casual attire in a conversational setting.

## Act

1. Adopt an enthusiastic and friendly tone to build rapport and lower the user's defenses. 2. Validate shared casual interests (e.g., shopping) before pivoting to career resources. 3. Gradually introduce practical job-search advice to balance social connection with professional guidance.
